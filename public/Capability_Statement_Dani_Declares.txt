DANI DECLARES LLC - GOVERNMENT CONTRACTING CAPABILITY STATEMENT

Corporate Identification & Core Government Capabilities

DANI DECLARES LLC provides execution-focused janitorial, facility-reset, and operational support services for commercial, institutional, municipal, and government environments. Government engagements are handled through procurement-specific scopes, solicitations, task orders, quotations, teaming arrangements, and subcontracting structures.

PRIMARY GOVERNMENT CAPABILITY
- Strategic target: NAICS 561720 — Janitorial Services
- Strategic target: PSC S201 — Custodial/Janitorial
- Federal registration status: Active SAM.gov registration verified in the owner's SAM.gov workspace

STRATEGIC ADJACENT CAPABILITY
- NAICS 561210 — Facilities Support Services

CORE PROCUREMENT-TRANSLATED CAPABILITIES:
- Institutional Janitorial Services: Recurring custodial cleaning, restroom/common-area service, trash/recycling support, and facility cleaning.
- Deep Facility Cleaning: Intensive cleaning and condition-based facility resets.
- Facility Turnover & Reset: Vacant-space, occupancy-transition, readiness, and move-in/move-out cleaning.
- Post-Construction Cleaning: Fine-dust, residue, adhesive, glass, and pre-occupancy cleaning.
- Facilities Operations Support: Scope-dependent work-order coordination, site scheduling, facility documentation, completion verification, and related operational support.
- Government Field Documentation: Photographic documentation, checklists, condition reporting, and completion evidence when required by the scope of work.
- Administrative / Documentation Support: Defined records, scheduling, task-tracking, contract-documentation, and closeout support when included in an eligible procurement scope.
