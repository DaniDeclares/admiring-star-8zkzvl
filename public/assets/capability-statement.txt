COMPANY OVERVIEW
DANI DECLARES LLC provides execution-focused janitorial, facility-reset, field documentation, and operational support for public-sector and institutional procurement. Government engagements are handled through procurement-specific scopes, solicitations, task orders, quotations, teaming arrangements, and subcontracting structures.

PRIMARY GOVERNMENT CLASSIFICATION

- PSC S201 — Custodial/Janitorial

STRATEGIC ADJACENT CLASSIFICATION
- NAICS 561210 — Facilities Support Services

CORE PUBLIC-SECTOR CAPABILITIES
1. CUSTODIAL: Recurring janitorial cleaning, deep facility cleaning, restroom/common-area service, trash/recycling support, and facility cleaning.
2. FACILITY RESET: Vacant-space, occupancy-transition, move-in/move-out, readiness, and post-construction cleaning.
3. FACILITIES SUPPORT: Scope-dependent work-order coordination, scheduling, site support, completion verification, and facility documentation.
4. FIELD DOCUMENTATION: Photographic documentation, checklists, condition reporting, and completion evidence when required by the contract scope.
5. ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT: Defined records, scheduling, task tracking, contract documentation, and closeout support when included in an eligible procurement scope.
6. SECONDARY / OPPORTUNITY-SPECIFIC: Supplies and materials, printing/signage, courier/delivery, and event/community support.

GOVERNMENT CONTRACTING PATH
