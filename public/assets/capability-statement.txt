COMPANY OVERVIEW
DANI DECLARES LLC provides execution-focused facility, field, administrative, logistics, supply and production support for public-sector and institutional procurement. Government engagements are handled through procurement-specific scopes, solicitations, task orders, quotations, teaming arrangements, and subcontracting structures.

CORE PUBLIC-SECTOR CAPABILITY FAMILIES
1. CUSTODIAL & FACILITY CARE
   Recurring janitorial, deep cleaning, facility resets, turnover/readiness, restroom/common-area service and trash/recycling support.
2. FACILITIES & SITE OPERATIONS
   Scope-dependent work-order coordination, scheduling, inspections, site support, condition documentation and completion verification.
3. EXTERIOR FACILITIES — OPPORTUNITY-SPECIFIC
   Pressure washing, exterior surface cleaning, gutters/downspouts, debris removal and exterior window cleaning when solicitation requirements can be met.
4. EMERGENCY SANITATION — CAPABILITY-GATED
   Emergency cleaning/sanitizing and incident-driven response; specialized remediation is performed only when independently qualified or through qualified partners.
5. SUPPLIES & DISTRIBUTION
   Facility/custodial supplies, consumables, equipment, sourcing, packaging, distribution and delivery support.
6. PRINTING & SIGNAGE
   Forms, manuals, signs, banners, labels, print-on-demand, print-distribution and graphic production through verified production capacity or qualified partners.
7. LOGISTICS & FIELD SERVICES
   Courier, delivery, pickup/dropoff, supply distribution, field documentation and site-support logistics.
8. ADMINISTRATIVE & DOCUMENTATION
   Records support, scheduling, task tracking, contract documentation, scanning, document preparation and closeout support when included in an eligible procurement scope.
9. PUBLIC EVENTS & COMMUNITY SUPPORT
   Event setup/breakdown, cleanup, signage, printed materials, supplies and logistics when specifically procured.
