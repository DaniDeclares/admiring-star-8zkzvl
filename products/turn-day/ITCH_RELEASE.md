## Short description
Turn a rough apartment into a rent-ready unit before the clock and budget run out.

## Page copy
You have one shift, four rooms, a limited budget, and QA waiting at the finish line.

Choose where to spend your time and money. Quick cleans are cheap, deep resets build quality, and repair-plus-clean moves can rescue a room at a cost. Get the whole unit lease-ready before your shift ends.

**Turn Day** is a tiny browser strategy/time-management game inspired by the real decisions behind property turns and field operations.

### Current build
- Four-room turn
- Time and budget constraints
- Multiple action strategies
- QA-style completion outcome
- Fast replay loop

This is the first public experiment from DANI DECLARES' digital product lab. Player feedback will determine what gets expanded next.
