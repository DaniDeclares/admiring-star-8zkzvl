## E. Business Model

DANI DECLARES monetizes through four channels:

1. **Direct B2C:** resident concierge and direct consumer services.
2. **B2B:** property management, real estate, commercial support, retainers, and facility operations.
3. **B2B2C:** community/resident programs distributed through partner properties.
4. **B2G:** federal/state/municipal/institutional procurement and prime-contractor teaming.

The economic objective is to move the revenue mix toward recurring B2B/B2G revenue while retaining higher-margin direct services and creative products as complementary cash-flow channels.

## F. Recurring Revenue Strategy

The company has an enterprise package layer for portfolio operations, real-estate executive support, volume turnover, enterprise facility support, and government/institutional task orders. These packages are quote/contract products and should not be treated as consumer checkout SKUs.

Target customer progression:

**single property → recurring property support → multi-property → portfolio operations → institutional / government.**

## G. Five-Year Asset Strategy

The current strategic objective is to use contract revenue and retained operating cash to build a durable asset base, eventually including land. The business plan identifies a Georgia family-compound concept as a Year-5 aspiration.

The financial model should therefore track three distinct cash buckets:

1. operating cash;
2. growth/equipment reserve;
3. long-term land/asset reserve.

No land purchase should be modeled as guaranteed until the actual cash-flow history, debt service, financing terms, and acquisition economics support it.
