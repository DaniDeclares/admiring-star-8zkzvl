# DANI Reconciliation Before Replacement

When multiple generations of a DANI subsystem exist, identify the current authority and reconcile older entry paths, records, receipts, and consumers into it where appropriate. Do not create a second active runtime merely to make older records visible.

Historical repositories and branches are evidence/reference unless live authority says otherwise. Carry forward unique still-valid behavior only after overlap and current-runtime checks. Retire or supersede obsolete paths explicitly so later agents do not revive them.
