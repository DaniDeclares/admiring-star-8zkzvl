# DANI DECLARES LLC — PROVIDER OPERATIONS SYSTEM

## Purpose

Turn the provider network into a controlled fulfillment system without giving away customer ownership, pricing authority, or operational IP.

## 1. Provider Lifecycle

**Candidate → Compliance File → Service Lane → Compensation Schedule → Work Order → Fulfillment → QC → Payout → Scorecard → Renewal/Restriction**

## 2. Provider File

Every active provider should have:

- legal name
- business name, if applicable
- W-9
- EIN/SSN as applicable
- service area
- phone/email
- emergency contact
- insurance evidence where applicable
- license/certification evidence where applicable
- authorized service lane
- compensation schedule
- approved payment method
- signed provider agreement
- expiration/renewal dates

## 3. Work Order Minimum Fields

- Work Order ID
- customer/account
- property/site
- service/capability ID
- requested date/time
- deliverables
- access instructions
- customer-facing price
- provider payout
- approved expenses
- materials treatment
- deadline
- evidence requirements
- escalation contact
- completion status

## 4. Financial Controls

The company owns the customer-facing price.

The provider receives the approved payout.

Internal formula:

**Customer Price − Provider Payout − Direct Materials − Payment/Transaction Costs − Approved Pass-Throughs = Contribution Margin**

A service should not be published until the economics are known.

## 5. Customer Ownership

Company-introduced customers remain DANI DECLARES customer relationships under the provider agreement. Providers must not accept direct payment for DANI-assigned services unless expressly authorized.

## 6. Data Security

Provider access follows least privilege. Sensitive financial/customer data is accessed only when required for the authorized service. MFA should be enabled where available. Credentials may not be shared. Confidential company information may not be uploaded to unauthorized AI systems.

## 7. Quality Control

Every field-service work order should support evidence appropriate to the service, such as:

- before/after photos;
- completion notes;
- materials used;
- exceptions/damage report;
- customer/site confirmation;
- timestamped completion.

## 8. Provider Scorecard

Track monthly:

- acceptance rate
- on-time rate
- completion rate
- customer complaints
- rework rate
- documentation compliance
- incident rate
- average payout
- gross margin contribution
- response time

## 9. Cass Financial Provider Lane

Cass's authorized lane is Business Financial Operations & Bookkeeping, including bookkeeping setup, reconciliation, financial-reporting organization, budgeting, cash-flow forecasting, break-even analysis, startup-cost organization, A/P and A/R tracking, funding-readiness packets, and financial accountability when separately authorized.

The relationship does not authorize CPA, audit/attestation, legal, investment, securities, or tax-representation services unless the required credentials and written authorization exist.

## 10. Fulfillment Rule

**No provider assignment without an authorized service lane.**

**No customer-facing promise without a work order.**

**No payout without completion evidence.**

**No direct customer payment unless explicitly authorized.**

**No access to systems beyond the minimum necessary.**
