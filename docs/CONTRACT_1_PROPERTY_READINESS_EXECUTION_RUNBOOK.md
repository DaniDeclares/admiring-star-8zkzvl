# DANI DECLARES — Contract #1 Property Readiness / Unit Turn Execution Runbook

**Status:** Working execution standard
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Objective

Close the first company contract through a single, evidence-controlled offer and carry it through discovery, proposal, agreement, fulfillment, invoicing, payment, proof and recurring expansion.

## Primary entry offer

**Property Readiness / Unit Turn Pilot**

Pricing model: scope-based, per-unit/per-property quote after discovery. Customer price must come from the authoritative DANI commercial pricing source. Public market pricing is research evidence only and provider retail pricing is never treated as provider payout.

## Scope layers

### Core
- Turnover cleaning/reset within approved non-trade scope
- Kitchen, bathroom, floor and serviceable-surface cleaning
- Ordinary service-related waste within approved scope
- Readiness/presentation check
- Completion documentation and exception reporting

### Optional add-ons
- Punch-list/property support where separately scoped and capability-approved
- Inspection/photo documentation
- Ordinary debris support where separately scoped
- Signage/readiness support
- Deep Unit Reset
- Recurring property support

### Expansion
Pilot → repeat turns → Property Turnover Program / Property Support Retainer → additional approved property services.

## Boundaries

No structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing or other licensed-trade repair unless separately scoped and appropriately authorized. No hazardous-material remediation, biohazard cleanup, pest treatment, major debris hauling or specialty restoration without separate qualification and authorization.

## Commercial sequence

1. Target account
2. Buyer identified/verified
3. Buyer conversation
4. Discovery
5. Confirm scope and site requirements
6. Validate authoritative DANI customer pricing
7. Confirm qualified/authorized fulfillment cell
8. Proposal
9. Client agreement/PO
10. Vendor onboarding requirements satisfied
11. Work order
12. Dispatch through canonical resolver
13. Fulfillment
14. Completion evidence + QA
15. Invoice
16. Payment reconciliation
17. Provider payable/payout record
18. Performance evidence
19. Recurring expansion proposal

## Dispatch gate

Do not dispatch until service scope, customer price, geography, provider capability, qualification/compliance, provider compensation, schedule, site requirements and evidence requirements are satisfied.

## CRM truth rules

- Target identified ≠ qualified prospect
- Named buyer ≠ engaged buyer
- Deal created ≠ sales opportunity
- Task created ≠ outreach completed
- Research completed ≠ pipeline advancement

Advance stages only when the corresponding real-world event is evidenced.

## Required evidence for Contract #1

- Buyer/contact evidence
- Discovery notes
- Proposal/version sent
- Client acceptance or executed agreement/PO
- Vendor onboarding requirements
- Provider qualification/compliance evidence
- Work order
- Completion evidence
- QA/acceptance
- Invoice/payment record
- Performance metrics/testimonial/reference where available

## Owner-only actions

- Legal signature/contract commitment
- Borrowing/debt commitment
- Account credentials or authorization only the owner can provide
- Physical-world outreach or relationship actions where not executed through an authorized system

Everything else should be completed through the available operating systems without unnecessary owner handoff.
