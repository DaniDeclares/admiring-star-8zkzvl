# Digital product quality assessment (2026-10-11)

Scope: every existing or recovered DANI digital product supplied by the owner (5 PDFs, 5 archives), plus the three drafts rebuilt in this branch. Method: objective audit (`tools/product-qa/audit_pdfs.py`: pages, real form fields, words/page, fonts, internal/placeholder terms) plus a visual read of page contact sheets in `products/digital/_quality-review/previews/`. **Nothing here replaces a customer download.** The Home Reset replacement is not approved. Longer is not scored as better.

Verdict key: KEEP (existing file is sound, no change needed) · IMPROVE (candidate work, owner decides) · NOT READY (do not sell as-is) · DRAFT (my rebuild, unapproved).

Cross-product findings:
- Only 2 of the 14 recovered PDFs carry real form fields; "Fillable Edition" in a file name is not proof of fillability (13 fields in the 14-page Job Kit).
- "fictional" and bracketed placeholders appear in customer-visible text in the marketing and House Manager kits; they need rewording before any sale, not deletion of the substance.
- Pocket apps (inspection, intake) make no network requests and store data only in the browser; that is a privacy strength to say plainly in listings.
- The premium cream/burgundy/gold pages (Cleaning, Holiday, Command Center, Addon) are the visual standard; the marketing kits and binder fall below it.
- Prices are unchanged everywhere ($19 / $15 / $9 where listed). No product was published or re-uploaded.
