# 1. FINAL STRUCTURAL DECISION

DANI DECLARES is an **Operational Asset Protection & Experience Infrastructure Company** whose commercial model may execute, coordinate, source, document, deliver, produce, or refer work across multiple divisions.

The company is therefore **not limited to work personally performed by Dani** and is not defined by any single provider, tool, division, channel, or current piece of equipment.

The catalog answers **what the company can legitimately organize and deliver**. Commercialization answers whether/how a capability is sold. Fulfillment answers who can perform it. Economics answer internal cost and payout. These layers remain separate.

---

# 2. NEW COMPANY-WIDE FULFILLMENT MODES — LOCKED

Every capability/service may eventually carry one or more of these fulfillment modes:

- `EXECUTE` — DANI or an authorized fulfillment provider performs the work.
- `COORDINATE` — DANI manages qualified third-party execution.
- `SOURCE` — DANI locates/procures the required resource or material.
- `DOCUMENT` — DANI performs field observation, evidence collection, reporting, or documentation.
- `DELIVER` — DANI or an authorized provider transports/deploys an item, document, asset, or supply.
- `PRODUCE` — DANI or an authorized provider creates a physical or digital output.
- `REFER` — DANI directs the customer to an appropriately qualified third party where DANI should not execute or coordinate the regulated work.

A capability may use multiple modes. Fulfillment mode is **not** a division, provider, SKU, price, or channel.

# 8. EQUIPMENT & INVENTORY CONTROL

Known equipment/capability must not be inflated into unsupported commercial claims.

Current catalog rule for carpet care:

- DANI has a Bissell machine.
- The Bissell must not be represented as a commercial carpet extractor without verification.
- Its hose is currently known to be broken.
- Carpet extraction therefore remains `PENDING_RECONCILIATION` / equipment-gated until the equipment is repaired/verified or an appropriate provider/equipment lane is secured.
- McCulloch MC1385 steam equipment is a separate available equipment capability.

Inventory establishes possible fulfillment capacity; it does **not** automatically authorize a customer-facing SKU.
