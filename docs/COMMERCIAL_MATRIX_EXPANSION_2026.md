# Commercial Matrix Expansion — Execution Ledger

## Historical Airtable Work / Current Research Boundary

Airtable was previously used during commercial architecture work for pricing evidence, price books, channel expansion, provider records and activation controls. Those records are preserved in the Airtable base but are now explicitly archived/non-active for operational use.

**Current rule:** Airtable is strictly a market-research system. Dedicated MARKET RESEARCH tables remain active for source-backed market pricing, company/competitor pricing evidence, public-sector opportunity intelligence, public-sector capability intelligence, and service-family market research. Operational/commercial control tables are archived and must not be used as current execution authority.

## Research integrity rule

Market evidence is not the same thing as DANI's approved customer price, provider payout, compliance status, or operational readiness. Research records may contain proposed/benchmark values for analysis, but those values do not authorize selling, dispatch, provider activation, customer fulfillment, or commercial catalog changes.

## Remaining activation gates

Commercial activation remains governed outside Airtable. Any service without verified fulfillment, compliance, SOP/QA, and approved execution economics remains gated. Provider prospects are not treated as qualified providers until evidence and agreements are actually verified.
