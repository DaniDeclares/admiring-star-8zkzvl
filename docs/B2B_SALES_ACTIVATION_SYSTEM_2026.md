## Sales Funnel

**Prospect → Qualification → Discovery → Scope → Quote/Proposal → Follow-Up → Close → Work Order → Fulfillment → Review → Retainer Expansion**

## Qualification Questions

1. How many properties/sites are in scope?
2. How many units/sites require recurring support?
3. What work is currently outsourced?
4. What is the biggest operational bottleneck?
5. What response time is required?
6. Who approves vendors?
7. What documentation/insurance is required?
8. What is the current vendor failure point?
9. Is the need recurring or project-based?
10. What would a successful first 30 days look like?

## Offer Strategy

Do not lead with the full catalog.

Lead with the customer's operational problem and one clear entry offer.

### Property manager entry offer

**Pilot Turn / Property Support Scope** → demonstrate execution → convert to recurring property support.

### Real-estate entry offer

**Listing & Transaction Support** → prove responsiveness → convert to monthly office operations support.

### Government prime entry offer

**Defined subcontracting work package** → prove documentation and field execution → pursue larger task orders.

## Proposal Requirements

Every proposal should contain:

- customer problem
- proposed scope
- exclusions
- deliverables
- service schedule
- price / pricing model
- materials/pass-through treatment
- customer responsibilities
- evidence/QC process
- acceptance method
- payment terms
- validity period
- next step

## First Commercial Objective

The goal is not maximum lead volume. The goal is the first group of customers that creates predictable recurring revenue and defensible operational proof.

Prioritize accounts that can generate repeated work, references, and expansion into portfolio-level relationships.
