# Shadow & Sol + Agricultural Operation — Business Plan Working Draft

**Planning document — not a representation to USDA, lender, donor or grantmaker.**

## Executive summary

The long-term concept combines a legitimate agricultural production operation with a separately governed nonprofit education/community mission and DANI DECLARES LLC's existing commercial capabilities. The objective is to develop a financially sustainable land-based operation that can support propagation/nursery production, food growing, conservation practices, agricultural education, workshops and community programming while maintaining strict legal/accounting separation between private, nonprofit and commercial uses.

The final farm borrower/operator has **not yet been selected or formed**. That decision will be made after FSA/NRCS, accounting, insurance, tax, land-control and underwriting review. Shadow & Sol is not presumed to own or operate the commercial farm merely because its programming uses agricultural land.

## Mission-aligned components

### Agricultural production
- plant propagation and nursery production;
- food/crop production appropriate to the selected land and market;
- season-extension/high-tunnel production where technically and financially justified;
- soil, water, habitat and conservation practices designed with NRCS/qualified advisors;
- future value-added products only after producer eligibility, food/product regulation and unit economics are established.

### Shadow & Sol nonprofit programming
- gardening and propagation education;
- land-based learning and field-school activities;
- workshops and practical skills education;
- nature, wellness and stewardship programming;
- community agriculture and food-system programming;
- beginning-farmer education only when qualified partners, curriculum and funding requirements are satisfied.

### DANI DECLARES commercial role
DANI remains a separate commercial services company. It may provide legitimate contracted admin, facilities, field, logistics, documentation, print/merch or other authorized services when procurement/conflict rules permit. DANI revenue is not nonprofit revenue, and nonprofit funds do not subsidize private/commercial activity without an allowable, documented transaction.

## Customers and markets — hypotheses to validate
- direct household plant/produce buyers;
- local/community market customers;
- institutions or businesses where product/service eligibility is proven;
- workshop/program participants through Shadow & Sol;
- nonprofit/community partners;
- future local-food distribution/value-added channels.

No demand or price assumption is application-authoritative until supported by research, quotes/contracts, comparable evidence or operating history.

## Property requirements

Screen candidate properties for:
- sufficient agriculturally usable acreage and water access;
- soil/topography/drainage appropriate to planned production;
- zoning permitting the intended agricultural uses;
- ability to separate private residence, commercial operation and nonprofit program areas;
- structures/infrastructure or buildability;
- road/logistics access;
- utility/internet needs;
- local market access;
- USDA/FSA/NRCS service-center coverage;
- rural-program eligibility where relevant;
- environmental/flood/wetland constraints;
- realistic acquisition, lease or seller-finance economics.

## Operations

Phase 0 — eligibility, planning and accounting readiness.
Phase 1 — secure qualifying land control and establish producer records/farm number as appropriate.
Phase 2 — minimum viable production and conservation plan; prioritize infrastructure that directly enables revenue or resource stewardship.
Phase 3 — expand propagation/production and Shadow & Sol programming with separate budgets and agreements.
Phase 4 — evaluate value-added, aggregation/direct-market and farmer-development programs based on demonstrated demand and compliance readiness.

## Management

Owner/operator management responsibilities, agricultural experience and training evidence remain **TBD/evidence-required** for FSA underwriting. Do not overstate experience. Build the gap-closing plan using education, Extension/USDA technical assistance, experienced producer partnerships and documented operating milestones.

## Financial plan

Financial model is deliberately not populated with invented values. It will draw from:
- canonical DANI accounting for DANI-only historical evidence;
- separate Shadow & Sol nonprofit accounting/fund restrictions;
- separate producer opening balance sheet and operating projections;
- property-specific purchase/lease terms;
- vendor quotes and USDA payment schedules;
- owner-approved contribution/equity assumptions.

Required model outputs: FSA-2037-style balance sheet, FSA-2038-style projected income/expense, monthly cash flow, sources & uses, debt service, break-even/sensitivity, grant-match schedule, capital asset schedule and inter-entity transaction schedule.

## Funding strategy

Use the right capital for the right use rather than expecting one grant to finance the compound:
- FSA ownership financing for eligible agricultural real estate/development;
- FSA operating financing for eligible farm operating needs;
- NRCS technical/financial assistance for qualifying conservation practices after eligibility/land-control gates;
- Shadow & Sol grants for allowable nonprofit/community programming;
- producer/local-food/value-added programs only when their specific eligibility and match rules are satisfied;
- private/residential costs funded separately from nonprofit/grant-restricted costs.

## Risks and controls

- **Eligibility risk:** official current program rules control; social-media claims do not.
- **Land-control risk:** no EQIP readiness claim without qualifying deed/lease and farm records.
- **Cash-flow risk:** grants may reimburse after spending; model timing and working capital.
- **Match risk:** never count unsupported in-kind/cash match.
- **Entity risk:** separate books, accounts, agreements and cost allocation.
- **Private benefit risk:** no nonprofit/grant funds for private residence or impermissible DANI benefit.
- **Debt risk:** no borrowing commitment without owner approval and repayment analysis.
- **Operational risk:** phase production rather than overbuilding before market proof.
