## Structural authority

Keep the roles separate:

- **Shadow & Sol nonprofit** — land-based education, gardening/propagation education, community agriculture, workshops, stewardship, community programming and skills education. Primary grant/program-development vehicle where the NOFO permits nonprofits.
- **DANI DECLARES LLC** — existing commercial services, facilities/logistics/admin/products and legitimate arm's-length contracted support. Do not convert DANI into a farm merely to chase funding.
- **Agricultural producer / operating entity — TO BE EVALUATED BEFORE FORMATION** — potential dedicated farm/nursery/production operator. Do not form or restructure until FSA/NRCS eligibility, accounting, insurance, tax and land-control consequences are reviewed.
- **Private residence/family property** — never represent private residential use as grant-funded nonprofit or agricultural program use.

No commingling. Any inter-entity services, occupancy, equipment use, cost allocation or reimbursement must be documented and priced/approved appropriately.

## Immediate clock

Georgia NRCS FY2027 first batching/ranking deadline for EQIP/CSP/ACEP is **2026-10-09**. Applications are accepted continuously, but current-cycle consideration uses state cutoff dates. Do not fabricate land control or a farm number to meet the date. If land-control prerequisites are absent, treat this as onboarding/preparation for a later ranking cycle.

NRCS financial-assistance eligibility commonly requires official tax ID, deed/lease showing control of property, and a farm number; FSA can issue the farm number. EQIP applications also require applicable conservation/eligibility certifications and a plan addressing a natural-resource concern.
