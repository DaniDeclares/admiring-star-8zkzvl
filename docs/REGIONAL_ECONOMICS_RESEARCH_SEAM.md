# Regional economics research seam

Future economics research should support a client-region selector without making browser geolocation mandatory.

## Research questions
- What geography level is reliable for each evidence source: ZIP, city, county, metro, state, country?
- Which costs vary materially by service location: labor, mileage/travel, disposal, materials, taxes/fees, provider supply, market price?
- What source freshness and sample-size thresholds are required before regional evidence can influence an economics proposal?
- How should remote/digital work differ from field-service geography?
- How should a client-entered service location override browser-derived location?

## Proposed boundary
Store normalized region as evidence metadata first. Browser location is optional convenience only. Explicit customer service address/location remains authoritative for fulfillment. Regional research may propose economics changes but cannot directly alter price or provider compensation.

This is research-only and intentionally does not add a webpage selector yet.
