# Danielle — Credentials & Capability Needs

A running list of certifications, licenses, or equipment Danielle needs to acquire before a
researched or requested service can move from "candidate" to a real, authorized, sellable SKU.
This exists because the catalog's real gating rule is authorization, not pricing (see the
Division 05 notary authorization work, 2026-09-17) — a service should never be marked
`SELL_NOW`/authorized until the underlying real-world capability actually exists.

## Open items

1. **Mobile fingerprinting certification** — needed to legitimately offer a Mobile
   Fingerprinting service alongside notary work (a common bundled offering for mobile notary
   businesses — background checks, licensing, immigration paperwork). Not certified as of
   2026-09-17. Do not add this as a catalog SKU until certification is obtained; once it is,
   it follows the same reconciliation pattern as everything else in Division 05 (scope it,
   price it with her real rate, authorize her in `dd_provider_capabilities`).

## Explicitly ruled out (not a "need," a hard blocker)

- **Remote Online Notarization (RON)** — Georgia does not currently permit RON under a GA
  notary commission. Not addable by acquiring a credential; would require a commission in a
  state that allows it, which is a bigger business decision, not a simple to-do item.
