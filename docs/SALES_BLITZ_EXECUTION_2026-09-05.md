# DANI DECLARES — Sales Blitz Execution Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Purpose:** Convert the current commercial architecture into a field-ready prospecting and contract-acquisition motion without rebuilding the catalog.

## 1. Operating objective

Secure the first recurring company/property contracts quickly, then use completed work to expand into recurring retainers and portfolio relationships.

The commercial path remains:

**Company → Buyer → Channel → Need → Offer → Contact → Status → Next Action → Proposal → Agreement/MSA → Payment → Work Order → Completion → Recurring**

No new customer channel is created.

## 3. Prospecting rule

Do not pitch the entire catalog in the first sentence.

Lead with the prospect's operational problem, then show the small set of services that solve it.

**Core positioning:**

> DANI DECLARES is a multi-service property, administrative, logistics and field-execution company. We help teams get the operational work around their core business handled without adding another full-time employee or another layer of coordination.

For property companies:

> You manage the property and resident relationship. We handle the execution work that has to happen on the ground — turns, resets, inspections, punch lists, packets, documentation, logistics and recurring property support.

For businesses:

> You run the business. We take recurring administrative and operational work off your plate so your team can stay focused on revenue and customers.

## 4. Blitz sequence

### Touch 1 — Direct introduction

Send a short email/contact-form message with one specific offer or workflow fit.

### Touch 2 — Same-day call

Call the business and ask for the person responsible for operations, property services, maintenance, office operations, or vendor partnerships.

### Touch 3 — Capability proof

Send the company capability/vendor packet and the relevant one-page service offer. Do not dump the full catalog unless requested.

### Touch 4 — Low-friction entry

Offer a first project, audit, unit turn, admin sprint, inspection/photo log, or other bounded engagement that can be completed without a long procurement cycle when the prospect's rules allow it.

### Touch 5 — Recurring conversion

After the first completed engagement, propose a recurring block/retainer based on the actual work pattern observed.

## 6. Contract target

The first contract does not need to be the largest contract.

Preferred progression:

**$395–$895 project → $795–$1,495 monthly relationship → $1,500–$4,500 recurring property/business relationship → portfolio expansion**

A small first project is commercially useful when it creates a path to recurring work.

## 7. Fulfillment guardrail

Only sell work DANI can personally execute or that has a qualified/authorized fulfillment path.

Do not promise provider capacity merely because a provider prospect exists.

Provider workflow remains:

**Permission → Qualification → Compliance → Agreement → Capability → Availability → Assignment**

## 8. Proposal/contract gate

Every qualified opportunity should move through:

**Discovery → Scope → Price/Quote → Proposal → Agreement/MSA → Payment/Deposit → Work Order → Completion → QA → Invoice/Payment → Recurring review**

Fixed approved services may use the existing estimate/quote builder. Variable, SOW, and contract/solicitation work stays quote/proposal based.

## 9. What is intentionally NOT a blocker

Do not delay the blitz for:
- Complete reconciliation of every catalog discovery row
- Every package being synchronized
- Every service having a channel-specific pricing rule
- Full provider-network authorization
- Perfect website photography across every route

Those remain parallel workstreams.

## 10. Immediate execution standard

Every prospect entered into the sales pipeline should have:

1. Company
2. Buyer/contact
3. Channel
4. Specific operational problem
5. Lead offer
6. Contact method
7. Status
8. Next action/date
9. Proposal requirement
10. Contract/payment path
11. Fulfillment owner
12. Follow-up outcome

The objective is not to accumulate leads. The objective is to create **qualified conversations that can become paid work**.
