# DANI DECLARES — GovCon Capability Expansion

**Effective:** 2026-09-10
**Status:** Approved / implementation in progress

## Purpose
Expand DANI DECLARES' government-contracting pursuit surface beyond janitorial without abandoning the existing GovCon strategy, evidence controls, or five-channel customer architecture.

## What remains locked
- Government/public-sector work remains **Channel 05 — Government & Institutional Procurement**.
- **NAICS 561720 / PSC S201** remain the primary strategic government lane.
- **NAICS 561210** remains a selective adjacent facilities-support lane.
- Georgia remains the immediate operating focus for commercial and near-term public-sector acquisition.
- Federal, state, county/municipal, public-authority, education, healthcare/institutional, prime/subcontract and teaming pathways remain valid.
- SAM, CAGE, NAICS/PSC, certifications, insurance, equipment, staffing, past performance and award history must not be presented as verified unless supported by authoritative current evidence.
- Airtable remains the GovCon research/intelligence layer; the application/Supabase remain the contract execution/runtime layer.

## Positioning rule
DANI leads with **what it can execute and document**, not an inflated list of classifications. Each pursuit is translated from the controlling solicitation into a qualification gate:

**Buyer → Facility/Requirement → Procurement Method → Scope → Classification → Eligibility → Equipment → Staffing → Insurance → Past Performance → Pricing → Submission Controls → Fulfillment Evidence**

## Prime / Sub / Team decision
- **PRIME:** use when DANI independently satisfies the solicitation's scope and qualification requirements.
- **TEAM/SUB:** use when DANI can perform a meaningful scope but a partner is required for specialized equipment, remediation, access, production capacity, geographic coverage, or other qualification gaps.
- **VENDOR/SUPPLIER:** use for product sourcing/distribution opportunities where compliant sourcing and delivery are demonstrated.
- **NO-BID:** use when the requirements cannot be responsibly satisfied.

## Presentation standard
Government-facing materials should:
- lead with the primary capability and procurement relevance;
- show adjacent capabilities as contractable, scope-dependent offerings;
- distinguish verified facts from strategic targets;
- avoid unsupported socioeconomic certification claims;
- avoid unsupported CAGE, current SAM classification, insurance, equipment, staffing, past-performance or award claims;
- show the execution model: scope → staffing/equipment → QA → evidence → reporting → invoice → closeout;
- make it easy for a buyer or prime contractor to identify where DANI fits.

## Current market proof points
The September 2026 GovCon watch identified live or upcoming examples spanning custodial, emergency sanitation, pressure washing/exterior facilities, institutional exterior cleaning, custodial supplies/distribution, printing, and federal facilities support. These examples are market intelligence, not DANI awards or past performance.

## Engineering boundary
Adding a capability to this document or to research does **not** automatically activate it for commercial sale, government proposal representation, provider dispatch, or fulfillment. Activation requires the relevant qualification, compliance, equipment, staffing, partner and operational gates to be satisfied.
