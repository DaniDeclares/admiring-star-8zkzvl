# DANI Research Memory + Opportunity Fabric

## Principle
Old does not mean irrelevant. Owner-originated Gmail/Drive material is durable candidate intelligence. It preserves provenance and intent, but it is not current authority merely because the owner authored it.

## Continuous loop
Authorized Gmail + attachments + Drive + revisions + web/official research + operational signals
→ immutable evidence/provenance
→ owner-memory candidate extraction
→ cross-signal matching against current services/products/providers/customers/operations
→ fresh external corroboration
→ economics + demand + capability + compliance + fulfillment research
→ tester proof
→ governed approval queue
→ production implementation/activation only after the applicable authority gate
→ outcome evidence back into research.

## First-class research programs
1. MERCH_PRODUCT_INTELLIGENCE
   - customer merchandise
   - property/real-estate/business buyer products
   - provider uniforms, field kits, ID/badges, equipment/consumables and optional provider-purchased supplies
   - welcome/onboarding/closing/event kits
   - DTF/apparel, sublimation, engraving, signage, NFC/QR, packaging/labels, printed collateral
   - xTool/other fabrication equipment and reseller/affiliate opportunities
   - product bundles and Business-in-a-Box concepts
   - demand, unit economics, labor, consumables, inventory/cash exposure, equipment amortization, safety, shipping, fulfillment and channel fit

2. PORTAL_UX_INTELLIGENCE
   - Owner HQ: actionable attention queue, today's operations, financial health, recent activity, schedule, pipeline, exceptions
   - Provider app: availability → offer → accept/decline/counter → job details → navigation → evidence/checklists → QA → earnings/payout
   - Customer portal: request/book, quote/estimate approval, appointments, job/evidence history, invoices/payments, messages, documents
   - Sales/dispatch/QA/accounting role views
   - permissions, accessibility, mobile/offline behavior, notification design, audit trails and progressive disclosure
   - research must compare evidence-backed workflows, not copy competitor visual branding

3. OWNER_RESEARCH_MEMORY
   - old business plans and business overviews
   - equipment research (including xTool)
   - past product/service concepts
   - funding/capital research
   - prior customer/provider research
   - old proposals, vendor packets, capability statements and pricing research
   - ideas embedded in emails, attachments, drafts and Drive
   - relevant historical claims are revalidated, not thrown away

## Cross-signal rules
- A Gmail/Drive idea that resembles an existing service/product is linked to it and checked for duplication, extension, bundle, pricing/economics or capability implications.
- A genuinely new service/product becomes a discovery candidate, not an active catalog item.
- Multiple origins can support one candidate: OWNER_ORIGINATED, CUSTOMER_DEMAND, PROVIDER_SIGNAL, MARKET_RESEARCH, COMPETITOR_PATTERN, GOVERNMENT/REGULATORY, OPERATIONAL_FRICTION.
- Freshness and authority are separate. Historical evidence can remain highly relevant while requiring current corroboration.
- Unsupported assertions found in historical research become claims-to-verify. They never become operational instructions by ingestion alone.

## Current external evidence examples (2026-09-26)
- xTool officially operates reseller and affiliate pathways; therefore the old reseller/affiliate concept is a valid current research candidate, while historical wholesale/margin figures still require current contract evidence.
- Current field-service products emphasize role-specific actionable home screens, customer self-service for booking/estimates/invoices/history, technician job context/evidence, and permissions. These are patterns to test against DANI workflows.
- PPAI/Oxford Economics reports a large U.S. branded-merchandise market; this supports continued merch-market research but does not validate any individual SKU or DANI margin.

## Authority
Research may observe, collect, classify, compare, synthesize, propose tests and create governed candidates. It may not publish pricing/products/services, authorize providers, spend money, contact external parties, merge/deploy production code, or change protected policy without the existing gate.
