## Certification Discipline

Do not market a socioeconomic certification unless it is current and supported by the relevant government record.

In particular, WOSB set-aside eligibility requires participation in the SBA WOSB Federal Contract Program; ordinary self-identification as women-owned is not equivalent to WOSB certification for set-aside competition.

## Opportunity Qualification Matrix

Every opportunity should be scored before bid:

- scope match
- NAICS/PSC match
- geographic feasibility
- contract size
- staffing requirement
- equipment requirement
- insurance/bonding requirement
- past-performance requirement
- certification requirement
- subcontracting restrictions
- payment terms
- margin potential
- mobilization cost
- incumbent/competition information
- deadline and bid effort

## Bid / No-Bid Rule

**BID** when scope fit, economics, staffing, compliance, and delivery risk are acceptable.

**TEAM/SUBCONTRACT** when the opportunity is larger than current capacity but the company has a defensible work package.

**NO-BID** when the work requires unsupported credentials, unavailable capacity, uneconomic travel, unacceptable liability, or unverified past performance.

## Government Page Claims Standard

Allowed:

- registered identifiers supported by company records;
- service capabilities actually offered;
- service areas actually supportable;
- current certifications supported by government records;
- substantiated experience.

Not allowed without evidence:

- "verified federal subcontractor"
- "WOSB certified"
- "8(a) certified"
- "HUBZone certified"
- specific contract awards
- past-performance claims that cannot be documented
- guaranteed set-aside eligibility
