## Purpose

This document is the authoritative company-wide catalog universe before SKU creation. It answers **what DANI DECLARES can do** across Divisions 01–13 without prematurely deciding customer prices, packages, provider payouts, or runtime IDs.

It replaces older service-directory taxonomies as the structural catalog authority. Older catalogs, spreadsheets, website generations, code registries, Stripe products, and pricing sheets remain preserved as historical evidence only unless an explicit reconciliation decision promotes an item.

## Non-Override Rules

1. Capability is not a provider.
2. Division ownership is not fulfillment ownership.
3. Capability is not a service, task, SKU, package, membership, retainer, or event contract.
4. A cross-division dependency does not create a duplicate service identity automatically.
5. No customer price is created by this document.
6. No provider payout or internal cost is created by this document.
7. No historical price is a fallback.
8. Missing scope, qualification, legal clearance, equipment, or economics remains `PENDING_RECONCILIATION`.
9. Division 13 is primarily an institutional contracting/wrapper layer; it does not duplicate underlying service SKUs merely because a government buyer purchases them.
10. All new SKUs remain frozen until Phase 1 reconciliation is complete.

# COMPANY-WIDE CROSS-DIVISION CAPABILITY REGISTRY RULES

## Core dependencies

- Cleaning can support property turns, real-estate readiness, institutional contracts, and household transitions without duplicating its core identity.
- Logistics can support any division requiring physical movement without becoming the owner of the underlying service.
- Marketing/media can support real estate, property operations, events, and business growth without changing division ownership.
- Creative production can support events, marketing, property operations, and business development without becoming the owner of those workflows.
- Administrative support can support every division without absorbing the division's core service.
- Notary remains Division 05 even when used in real-estate or government workflows.
- Computer repair/setup remains Division 06 even when Chris fulfills it.
- Event planning/production remains Division 10 even when marketing, logistics, creative production, or staffing are dependencies.
- Government procurement remains Division 13's wrapper while underlying work remains structurally classified in its originating division.
