# DANI DECLARES LLC — MASTER EXECUTION PLAN

**Effective:** 2026-08-20  
**Status:** Operating-company completion plan  
**Purpose:** Convert the existing DANI DECLARES strategy, pricing architecture, provider network, government lane, technology platform, and five-year legacy plan into a controlled operating system.

## 1. North Star

DANI DECLARES is an execution company. The commercial engine is:

**Content + visibility → qualified demand → standardized offers → recurring B2B/B2G revenue → controlled provider fulfillment → documented margins → retained cash → equipment/assets → land → legacy infrastructure.**

The company should not add complexity merely for appearance. Every new division, offer, provider, or technology feature must strengthen one of five outcomes:

1. cash generation;
2. recurring revenue;
3. operational leverage;
4. contract readiness; or
5. asset/land accumulation.

## 5. Provider Operating Model

DANI DECLARES controls the customer-facing relationship, service packaging, customer-facing pricing, intake, scheduling, payment collection, and work-order assignment. Providers remain independent businesses and perform only authorized service-lane work.

Provider requirements:

- W-9
- legal/business identity
- service area
- insurance where applicable
- licenses/certifications where applicable
- compensation schedule
- approved work order
- incident reporting
- data-security compliance
- no unauthorized subcontracting
- no circumvention of company-introduced customers

Provider economics must be maintained as **customer price ≠ provider payout**. Every service should have an internal cost ceiling and a target gross-margin contribution before publication.
