# Shadow & Sol Research Staging

## Purpose

Shadow & Sol is a separate intended nonprofit organization. Its Supabase project `yxhbernpesgzvfdoubyd` is currently inactive, so nonprofit research is temporarily staged in the DANI tester only. This is a research bridge, not an operational database merger.

## Boundary

- DANI production is not a target.
- Shadow & Sol customer/donor/participant/financial/volunteer data is not stored here.
- No filing, payment, grant submission, contract, compensation, property action, or public claim is authorized by this staging layer.
- Every staged Shadow & Sol record carries an entity scope and target-project context.
- Existing mixed `ENTITY_ARCHITECTURE` research is classified as `DANI_ONLY`, `SHADOW_SOL_ONLY`, or `CROSS_ENTITY`.

## Foundation research programs

The staging seed creates nonprofit-native research programs for formation/governance, field-school and learning-experience classification, 365-day seasonal R&D/curriculum, mission programs, property/facilities, grants/capital, nonprofit finance/earned revenue, people/youth safety/risk, DANI related-party architecture, GA/SC geography, technology/data/security, brand/public trust, and research self-governance.

The program model explicitly covers the concepts already discussed for Shadow & Sol: land-based education, gardening and propagation, herbal/plant education, stewardship, nature and wellness-oriented community learning, field-school/workshop experiences, family/youth/adult learning, seasonal field dossiers, kits/components, and potential glamping/overnight educational experiences. These are research subjects, not approved programs.

## Evidence governance

Automated source watches are discovery infrastructure only.

A source-watch keyword/signal match MUST NOT create confirmed authority. The research runner now stores current automated matches as `PARTIAL` observations requiring reconciliation. Non-2xx HTTP responses are source failures, not healthy checks.

The live tester remediation downgraded previously automated `CONFIRMED` rows to `PARTIAL`. The companion migration preserves that fail-closed rule in source control.

## High-impact action boundary

Research may run automatically. It may not automatically:

- file or submit applications;
- move or spend funds;
- sign contracts;
- establish compensation;
- appoint or remove governance;
- acquire or lease property;
- publish regulated claims;
- change entity authority; or
- approve related-party transactions.

## Litha and seasonal prototype material

Existing seasonal dossier material is registered as user-source prototype evidence only. Its existence, structure, and internal framing can inform R&D. Historical, botanical, wellness, spiritual-tradition, safety, legal, and similar substantive claims still require independent evidence before they are treated as externally verified.

## Migration target

When `shadow-and-sol` is restored, move only entity-scoped Shadow & Sol research architecture/data into that project through a reviewed, idempotent migration. Do not copy DANI customer, provider, commercial, or financial operational data.
